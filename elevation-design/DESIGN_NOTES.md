# Valamil Residence — Elevation Design

Two-storey elevation proposal derived from the uploaded ground and first floor plans.

## Plan summary

| Level | Key spaces |
| --- | --- |
| **Ground** | Drawing (13'-9" × 23'-6"), Kitchen/Dining (14'-9" × 13'-9"), Pooja (5'-0" × 4'-0"), Master bedroom (20'-6" × 14'-0"), Toilet (13'-9" × 5'-0") |
| **First** | Sit-out (28'-3½" × 13'-9"), 2 bedrooms (~14' × 14'), 2 toilets, North balcony 6'-6", West balcony 4'-0" |
| **Footprint** | Building C/C ≈ 35'-9½" × 29'-3" (~36'-6" × 30'-0" overall) |
| **Site** | Corner plot — North Road + East Road; parking in 9' east setback; external stair on east |

## Elevation concept

**Modern tropical contemporary** — cream plaster, charcoal accent bay, teak frames, stone-clad stair tower, flat RCC terrace with parapet.

### North elevation (front / North Road)
- Ground: Drawing-room vertical fenestration as the main street face
- First: Continuous sit-out balcony (6'-6") with sliding glass and horizontal railing
- West wrap of the 4'-0" balcony reads as a light return on the left
- Stair/porch mass held to the east edge so the north face stays calm and residential

### East elevation (East Road)
- Porch canopy over car bay on square columns
- External U-stair expressed as a vertical tower (stone + open landings)
- Kitchen openings near stair; master-bedroom windows toward the south end
- Secondary street presence for the corner plot

### Corner perspective
Northeast view combining both road-facing facades for a client-ready massing check.
