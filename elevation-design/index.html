How the plan was read

The sheets are rotated: on the plan, North Road is on the left and East Road is on top (the north arrow points left). Earlier concept images had the sides mirrored.

Orientation

  • Plot 45' (north-south) x 42' (east-west), corner plot
  • Building 36'-6" (north-south) x 30'-0" (east-west)
  • Setbacks: east 9' (car park + stair), west 4', south 3'
  • Gates are on the North Road side

Ground floor (as per plan)

  • North wall: entry door and one Drawing window
  • East wall: main entrance, Drawing window, Pooja window, Kitchen window
  • South wall: kitchen service door, dining window, master bedroom window
  • West wall: toilet window and two master bedroom windows

First floor

  • Sit-out opens to the 4'-0" north balcony through an opening about 10'-0" wide, drawn as sliding glass doors
  • Sit-out also opens to the 6'-6" east balcony (about 7'-5" opening) plus two windows
  • Bedrooms and toilets keep their plan windows on the south and west

Each elevation has an alignment sheet: the plan is rotated so the facade faces you, and dashed lines run from every opening on the plan up into the elevation (GF blue, FF orange).

Assumptions to confirm

The plans give plan positions only, so these items are design choices rather than plan data.

Heights

  • Plinth 1'-6", floor-to-floor 10'-6" (12'-0" first floor level)
  • Terrace slab at 22'-0", 3'-0" parapet
  • Windows 3'-0" sill to 7'-0" head; toilet windows 4'-6" sill
  • Stair headroom cover rises to 30'-6"

Covered stair

  • Footprint taken from the plan: 14'-4" x 6'-6" (including its 8" north wall) at the south end of the east side, flush with the south wall and the balcony edge
  • Entry door and first floor landing door are on the stair's north face, onto the car porch and the east balcony
  • Light slots and a head ribbon are design additions

Things the plan raises

  • The kitchen window (east wall) now opens into the covered stair, so it needs a vent or jali there
  • The plan shows the sit-out east and north openings as folding doors; both are drawn as sliding glass
  • The east balcony label says 6'-6"; the drawn hatch measures about 6'-1". The label was used.
